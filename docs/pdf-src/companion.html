Different market, same job.

Why Lifepal's insurance marketplace, consumer and B2B, maps onto Codat's business-data platform.

Both businesses sit in the middle. Lifepal connected people and SMBs with the insurers that cover them; Codat connects SMBs' accounting, ERP, banking and commerce data with the lenders, banks and platforms that serve them. In both, many partners describe the same thing differently, data changes after the fact, each party may only see what it was shown, and a real financial decision depends on the number being right. The data problems that follow are the ones NEXUS was built to solve.

What carries over directlyproven at Lifepal

One definition per metric

Lifepal: agreeing "bind" took four workshops; ad-hoc requests then fell 60%.

Here: revenue and cash flow agreed once, in a contract, before any API or dashboard.

Isolation by policy

Lifepal: a row access policy scoped every insurer to its own data.

Here: each customer sees only consented companies; agents inherit it.

Late data, measured

Lifepal: lookback set at 72 hours, the p99.5 of partner delay.

Here: lookback sized from real re-sync and backdating lag per source.

History, not overwrite

Lifepal: changing attributes kept as SCD2, joined at the time they applied.

Here: account remaps can't rewrite last month's revenue.

Tests before trust

Lifepal: 500+ checks in production; data-trust escalations −60%.

Here: every contract compiles into checks; the bank break stays red.
