03 · Built in Lifepalconsumer and B2B

Different market, same job.

Lifepal sat between people and SMBs and the insurers that cover them. Codat sits between SMBs' finance systems and the lenders and platforms that serve them. The data problems are the same.

Lifepal B2B · SMB → insurers

Collect once, serve many insurers

A small business shared its company and team data once, with consent. Several insurers, each with its own format, quoted on it, and statuses came back late, for days.

  • Mapped to each insurer by contract, not by hand
  • Each insurer saw only the SMBs that applied to it
  • Newest status wins; a 72-hour lookback from measured delay
  • "Bind" agreed once; ad-hoc requests −60%
Codat · SMB → lenders and platforms

Connect once, decide on one number

An SMB connects its accounting or ERP, bank and commerce accounts once. Each customer it shares with needs one trustworthy view, while the books keep changing.

  • Source contracts map each system onto one canonical model
  • Consent as data: one row filter on every product and tool
  • Re-syncs and backdated credit notes never double count
  • Revenue and cash flow defined once, for people and agents
In Lifepal, the other side was the insurers. At Codat it's the lenders, banks and platforms. Same job: each one sees only the businesses that chose to share with it, and all of them get one agreed number.
